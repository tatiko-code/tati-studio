Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Tati Studio ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website tatibridal.com and the Tati Bridal Scheduler application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you submit (name, email, phone) when booking an appointment or contacting us.
  • Usage data collected automatically (pages visited, browser type, device type) via standard web server logs.
  • If you connect a social media account (Instagram, TikTok) through our scheduling tool, we access only the permissions you explicitly grant.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to appointment requests and inquiries.
  • To schedule and publish content to your connected social media accounts on your behalf.
  • To improve our website and services.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share data with trusted service providers (e.g., email services) strictly to operate our business. We comply with TikTok's Platform Terms and Developer Policies regarding any data accessed via TikTok APIs.

4. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@tatistudio.com.

6. Cookies

We use minimal cookies necessary for the website to function. No third-party advertising cookies are used.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related questions: hello@tatistudio.com
